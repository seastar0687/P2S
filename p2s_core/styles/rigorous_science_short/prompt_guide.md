# Rigorous Science Short Prompt Guide

This style turns one scientific paper into a short, careful explainer. It should feel concise and watchable, but never more certain than the paper.

## Structure

1. Hook: a paper-supported question or tension.
2. Problem: what gap the paper addresses.
3. Method intuition: what the authors try.
4. Key result: the most important reported finding.
5. Limitation: where the evidence stops.
6. Takeaway: a careful summary.

## Writing Rules

- Keep voice lines short.
- Prefer concrete verbs.
- Use one claim per scene.
- Tie important claims to evidence.
- Mention uncertainty before the ending if the result is narrow.

## Avoid

- Absolute claims.
- Unsupported real-world implications.
- Sensational contrast.
- Phrases listed in `forbidden_phrases.txt`.
