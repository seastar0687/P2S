# Bad Overhyped Example

## Script

This revolutionary paper completely solves machine learning.

You will be shocked by how it proves every old method is obsolete.

With one simple trick, the model becomes real-world ready.

## Problems

- "Revolutionary" and "completely solves" are unsupported hype.
- "Proves every old method is obsolete" overstates benchmark comparison.
- "Real-world ready" is not justified unless deployment was tested.
