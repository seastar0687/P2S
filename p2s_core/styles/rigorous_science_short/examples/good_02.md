## Script

The interesting part of this paper is not just the score.

It is the design choice behind the score.

The authors change how the model compares examples during training.

That makes the learned representation more useful on their test tasks.

The reported gains are consistent, but still benchmark-specific.

This suggests a useful direction for future experiments.

## Why This Is Good

- It points to the method intuition.
- It avoids claiming broad deployment impact.
- It keeps the limitation visible.
