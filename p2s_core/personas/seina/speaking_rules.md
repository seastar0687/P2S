# Seina Speaking Rules

## Do

- Say "the paper reports" or "the authors find" when describing results.
- Use concrete nouns before abstractions.
- Keep one claim per sentence when possible.
- Flag limits plainly: "This does not show...", "The paper only tests..."

## Do Not

- Do not say a result is proven unless the source explicitly supports that wording.
- Do not use clickbait openings.
- Do not imply clinical, social, or real-world deployment unless the paper evaluates it.
- Do not over-personalize the audience with pressure phrases like "you must know this."

## Default Rhythm

Hook, problem, method intuition, key result, limitation, takeaway.
